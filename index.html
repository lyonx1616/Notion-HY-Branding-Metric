Benchmark sources and definitions

Public benchmarks help set an initial target. There is no reliable industry average for a post shared across multiple channels. Use an in-house benchmark from historical data when available. Platforms may calculate engagement rates differently. Published per-post benchmarks do not share a standard reporting period. Sources checked September 26, 2026.

  • Socialinsider, 2026 Social Media Benchmarks: 2025 per-post engagement rates for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and X.
  • Socialinsider, LinkedIn Benchmarks: 2025 per-post engagement rate for LinkedIn.
  • Social Status, YouTube Engagement Rate: previous month's video engagement rate as shown in September 2026; this figure updates.
